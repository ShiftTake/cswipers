Escrow Framework

CardSwipers uses Stripe Connect Separate Charges and Transfers to temporarily hold buyer funds on the CardSwipers Stripe platform account for up to 90 days while shipment and delivery are verified. CardSwipers is not a bank, money transmitter, trust company, or licensed escrow agent. The platform only administers a software-enabled payment holding and release workflow.

48-Hour Inspection Window

Once the shipping carrier marks an order as delivered, the buyer has exactly 48 hours to inspect the package and either accept delivery or open a dispute. If the buyer takes no action within that 48-hour window, CardSwipers will automatically and irrevocably release the held funds to the seller, and the transaction will be treated as fully completed.

Limitation of Liability

CardSwipers is not liable for lost, stolen, delayed, misdelivered, or damaged packages, for shipping carrier outages or tracking inaccuracies, or for fraud, counterfeits, misrepresentation, or other misconduct by users. CardSwipers does not guarantee item authenticity, condition, value, or merchantability. To the fullest extent permitted by law, CardSwipers' aggregate liability for any escrow transaction is capped strictly at the 2% platform fee collected from the buyer on that transaction.

Binding Dispute Consent

By using CardSwipers escrow checkout, both buyer and seller agree that CardSwipers administrators may review shipment data, tracking history, dispute explanations, and related transaction records to decide whether funds should be refunded to the buyer or released to the seller. Both parties agree that CardSwipers' dispute resolution decision is final and binding for purposes of the platform workflow, and both parties waive claims, chargebacks, or legal actions against CardSwipers arising from the administration or outcome of a dispute, except where such waiver is prohibited by law.

User Obligations

Sellers must ship accurately described items using valid tracking details. Buyers must provide accurate shipping information and inspect delivered packages promptly. Any attempt to abuse the escrow flow, submit false disputes, misrepresent inventory, or evade payment obligations may result in account suspension or permanent removal.
